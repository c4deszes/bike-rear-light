Mechanical requirements
=======================

.. req-sys:: Seatpost mounting specification
    :id: SYS_REQ_ME_SEATPOST_SPEC
    :status: accepted
    
    The rear light shall be attachable to a seatpost with a diameter of ``31.6mm``.

.. req-sys:: Seatpost mounting mechanism
    :id: SYS_REQ_ME_SEATPOST_MOUNT
    :status: accepted
    :tags: security, safety

    The rear light shall not be detachable by hand, it should require some tool to make theft more
    difficult.
