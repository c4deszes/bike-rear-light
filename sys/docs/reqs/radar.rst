Radar requirements
==================

.. req-sys:: Radar minimum range
    :id: SYS_REQ_RADAR_MIN_RANGE
    :status: draft

    The radar shall detect targets up to 10m from it's mounting position.

.. req-sys:: Radar speed detection
    :id: SYS_REQ_RADAR_SPEED_RANGE
    :status: draft

    The radar shall have a relative speed detection capability of:

    * 5km/h for departing targets
    * 40km/h for approaching targets

.. req-sys:: Radar mounting
    :id: SYS_REQ_RADAR_MOUNTING
    :status: accepted

    The radar shall be mounted at a 3.5° offset to the left or have the sensitivity adjusted to
    focus on targets approaching from the left.
